How it works
  1. Photo. Take or upload a picture of the front of your house.
  2. Size. Drag straight across a garage door or the front door, from the left edge to the right edge.
  3. Lights. Pick a tool and a color, then trace where you want lights.
  4. Estimate. Check the price range. It’s an estimate until we review it.
  5. Send. Enter your info and send. We’ll text you a final quote.
